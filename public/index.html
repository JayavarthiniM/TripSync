How the planner decides

Hard constraints: fixed bookings, trip-day boundaries and the available time window. Soft preferences: group interests, low walking, lower estimated cost and preserving original stops. The app should explain infeasible schedules rather than pretending every request can be satisfied.

Worldwide place discovery is powered by community-mapped OpenStreetMap data. Results vary by location and category. Please respect the usage policies of geocoding, map tile and place-query services; for larger usage, use a suitable provider or host your own data service.
